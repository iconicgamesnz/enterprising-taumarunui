Privacy and transparency

How Enterprising Taumarunui handles information sent through this website.

Contact us

Information you send

ETI uses your name, email and message to respond to enquiries. Membership applications also ask for a membership type and, where relevant, the organisation name. Netlify Forms stores these submissions for authorised project administrators.

Events and directory listings

Event reviewers can see the organiser email and proposed event details. Approved titles, descriptions, times and venues appear on Google Calendar and the website. Keep personal details out of the public description. ETI keeps submissions and decisions in its private Netlify event store.

Directory listings show public business contact details. ETI can correct a listing or remove it from the directory.

Access, correction and removal

Email info@taumarunui.co.nz to ask about information held about you, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. Include the listing, form or event name so ETI can locate it.

Netlify hosts the site. Opening the calendar connects your browser to Google. These providers may process technical information such as your IP address.
