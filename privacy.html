Privacy statement

How Enterprising Taumarunui handles information sent through this website.

Contact us

Draft for committee review — applies to the proposed replacement website. The current official website is taumarunui.co.nz.

Who we are

Enterprising Taumarunui Incorporated (ETI) provides community event information, business listings and membership information. Contact ETI about privacy at info@taumarunui.co.nz.

Information we collect and why

When you submit an event, we collect the event title, description, dates, location, optional poster and registration links, and organiser email address. We use these details to assess the listing, contact the organiser if needed and publish approved events. Providing the required details is voluntary, but without them we cannot process an event submission. The organiser email field is not included in the public event feed. Do not include private contact details in the public description.

The enquiry form is disabled by default. If enabled after approval, it collects your name, email and message for authorised dashboard review, without automated email notifications. Existing membership and float registration links open separate Google Forms. If you choose to submit those forms or email ETI, the information you provide is handled through those channels for the relevant enquiry, application or registration.

Public listings and images

The directory may include publicly available business names, locations, contact details and services. Directory inclusion does not establish ETI membership. You can request corrections or removal. Submit only images you have permission to share, and remove embedded location metadata or unnecessary personal information before sharing them.

Hosting, cookies and external services

The proposed website uses Netlify for hosting and storage. Hosting and security services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs to operate and protect the website. The private preview may use access-control cookies. The replacement does not offer Gravatar profiles or public user accounts. Optional comments are disabled by default; if enabled, display names and comments are reviewed before publication. Do not include private details in public comments.

Google Maps embeds, Google Fonts, links to Google Forms and externally hosted images may connect your browser to other providers, which may process technical information or use cookies under their own policies. See Google’s privacy policy and Netlify’s privacy policy. Optional calendar subscriptions or a Google calendar mirror may share approved public event information; the mirror is not enabled by default.

Access, storage and disclosure

Event submissions and review decisions are stored in the website's Netlify data store and are intended to be accessible only to authorised reviewers and project administrators. Approved public event and business details are visible to visitors. Hosting and other service providers may process information outside New Zealand. Before public launch, ETI must confirm its arrangements for storage, access and overseas handling of personal information.

How long information is kept

ETI should retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or to meet applicable legal obligations. A retention and deletion schedule for event submissions and review records must be confirmed before public launch.

Your privacy rights

Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020, you can request access to personal information held about you and ask for it to be corrected. You may also request removal; this will be considered in light of the purpose for retaining the information and applicable obligations. Email ETI with enough information to identify the relevant record. You can raise concerns with ETI or contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes to this statement

ETI will need to review this statement before launch and whenever collection practices, integrations or services change.
