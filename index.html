Discover our place

Welcome to Taumarunui!

Our place has been loved by generations of families for our naturally beautiful environment, our colourful heritage and our caring community.

Located on State Highway 4 at the start of the Forgotten World Highway, Taumarunui is a gateway to Whanganui National Park, Tongariro National Park and the Pureora Timber Trail. Explore the rivers, cycling trails, fishing, walks, rail cart adventures and scenic landscapes.
